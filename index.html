Built to respect your device.

No root, no bypassing

ADBTweaks uses Android’s own Wireless Debugging and Developer tools. It does not bypass thermal limits, carrier restrictions, or OEM policies. Results vary by Android version, manufacturer, and firmware.

Your data stays on-device

Settings, device identities, baselines, game profiles, history, and diagnostic reports are stored locally. Reports are shared only when you choose a share or send action. No account, no ad profiles.

Use it on your own devices

Wireless ADB and developer authorization are required, and the target must approve the connection. Use ADBTweaks only on devices you own or are authorized to administer.

Reversible where possible

Standard tweaks capture baselines and offer restore. Package actions require confirmation, protected packages stay unavailable, and long-running operations show progress and results.

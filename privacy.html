Privacy Policy

Effective date:

This policy explains how the ADBTweaks website handles the information you provide when you register to be notified about the ADBTweaks pre-release. It applies only to this website and the signup form. The Android app itself is covered by the separate Privacy Policy packaged inside the app.

1. What we collect

When you use the pre-release registration form, we ask for:

  • Email address — required, so we can notify you when a pre-release build is ready.
  • Target Android version — optional; helps us prioritize compatibility testing.
  • Device model — optional; used only to prioritize testing against the devices people use.

We do not ask for, and do not require, a name, account, or password.

2. How we use it

Your information is used only to:

  • Notify you when the ADBTweaks pre-release is available to install, and
  • Occasionally understand the Android versions and devices that registrants plan to use so we can test them.

We do not use your data for advertising, for building advertising profiles, or for anything unrelated to the ADBTweaks release.

3. Who processes it

Submissions are collected through Formspree, a form-processing service. Formspree stores the submission data on their infrastructure and is the data processor for the purposes of this form. See Formspree’s privacy policy for details on their storage and security practices.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your information. It is shared only with:

  • Formspree, as the form processor described above, and
  • Service providers needed to send the release notification emails, if the email delivery is handled by a third-party mailing service.

5. Retention

Registration data is kept for as long as it is useful for distributing the pre-release and its updates. If you want your registration removed from our list, contact us using the details below and we will remove it promptly.

6. Security

The form is submitted over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection, and Formspree applies access controls and monitoring to the data it stores. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your rights

You may at any time:

  • Ask what information we hold about you,
  • Ask us to correct or delete it, or
  • Unsubscribe from release notifications.

To do any of these, contact us using the details below.

8. Children

This website and the ADBTweaks app are not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page will be updated when it changes. Continued use of the website or the signup form after changes means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy or requests to access or delete your registration can be sent through the ADBTweaks update channel: @mister_p27official on YouTube.
